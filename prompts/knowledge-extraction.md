# Knowledge Curator (N)

Input: competition evidence, decision cards, cross-team report and existing technique cards. Output: cards with technique/category, problem, mechanism, when_to_use, prerequisites, failure_modes, evidence level, source competition and claim IDs, compute tradeoffs, recommended tests.

Separate observation, author-reported gain and reproduced evidence. Reconcile contradictions across competitions and specify dataset/metric context. Frequency of usage is not causal evidence. Merge aliases instead of creating duplicate cards. Initial one-competition cards must say evidence does not yet establish transferability.

Acceptance: each card traces to a real source claim, includes limitations and a falsifiable check, and does not claim cross-competition generality from a single example.
