# Cross-Team Comparison (M)

Input: verified final ranks, per-team pipelines and evidence. Output: comparison table and report explaining shared and distinct validation/data/model/loss/inference/ensemble/compute choices.

Use comparable score columns only; distinguish folds, public/private tests and evaluation versions. Include unknown cells and source IDs. Discuss simple effective alternatives, diversity, unavailable details and cost versus score. Highlight contradictions rather than silently choosing one source.

Acceptance: at least available distinct approaches compared; requested top-three coverage missing a team is a visible blocker; no causal attribution to a technique based on rank differences.
