  <div class="node-content">
    
            <div class="field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field-item"><img src="/sites/default/files/inline-images/image_16.png" data-entity-uuid="111e9174-b49a-44df-8e16-ec2017bf4398" data-entity-type="file" alt width="512" height="512" class="align-center" loading="lazy"><p>The VLSP 2026 evaluation campaign deals with several shared tasks for text and speech processing (<a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/registration">Shared Task Registration Forms</a>).</p><ol><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/sred">Speaker Recognition on Edge Device&nbsp;</a> &nbsp;&nbsp;</li><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/vinvtts">Vietnamese Non-Verbal Text-to-Speech for Conversational Synthesis</a>&nbsp; &nbsp;</li><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/sqa">Speech Quality Assessment</a>&nbsp; &nbsp;</li><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/vgec">Vietnamese Grammatical Error Correction Challenge</a></li><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/spartqa">Vietnamese Spatial Reasoning on Textual Question Answering</a></li><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/vipra2026">Vietnamese Physics Reasoning and Answering Challenge</a></li><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/vitnumchart">Numerical Reasoning Question Answering</a>&nbsp; &nbsp;</li><li><a href="https://vlsp.org.vn/vlsp2026/eval/mt_challenge">Document-Level Legal Machine Translation with Limited-Parameter Models</a>&nbsp; &nbsp;</li></ol><p>VLSP shared tasks aim to promote the most efficient methods for these important tools. The organization of these campaigns with sponsorships from academia and industry permits to building and offering to the VLSP community gold datasets for training and testing Vietnamese text and speech processing systems.</p><p>Participants in the evaluation campaign will be asked to present their system in a dedicated paper.</p><p>&nbsp;</p></div>
